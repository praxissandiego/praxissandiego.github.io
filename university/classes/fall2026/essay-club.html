Course Description

This class is an essay club, specifically an essay-reading club, with a particular theme this term, in short, "Thinking". Think of it as a normal reading club... but with essays. Essay Club is for you if you want a little more lively discussion in your life, and this term is particularly for you if you have an interest in understanding the world around us, the way it works, and using that as a lens for decisionmaking and meaning-finding.

The class format will look as follows: getting settled in, while we wait for everyone to show up. Then, we'll talk about the essay, see if anyone had strong emotions, takes, disagreements with the text.

The essays for this term all center around the following themes: how can one understand the world, what traps does thinking lead us to, and perhaps even a call to arms for being more serious.

These are the essays we will read this term:

  • Eliezer Yudkowsky, “Making Beliefs Pay Rent (in Anticipated Experiences)”, Oct 25th
  • David Chapman, “How To Think Real Good”, Nov 1st
  • Scott Alexander, “Meditations on Moloch”, Nov 8th
  • Visakan Veerasamy, “Are you serious?”, Nov 15th
